\section{Introduction}

Formal Property Verification (FPV) establishes whether a SystemVerilog design satisfies a SystemVerilog Assertion (SVA). Its cost, however, grows quickly with the size of the design's state space. Verification engineers commonly use two complementary strategies to control this cost:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Hierarchical lemma generation:} construct a DAG of assertions rooted at the target, with helper assertions as descendants. Assuming proved child properties should make each parent assertion easier to verify, which decomposes a difficult proof into a sequence of tractable ones.
    \item \textbf{State-space reduction:} introduce cutpoints, abstractions, and blackboxing to shrink the space the verifier must explore.
\end{itemize}

We propose an LLM agent that interacts with the verifier in a closed loop. At each step, it adds helper assertions, cutpoints, abstractions, or blackboxes, then uses the verifier's response to choose its next action.

We ask whether such agent can reduce the time or compute needed to prove valid SVA properties compared with direct proving and verifier-feedback prompting. We also test whether it can prove properties that these baselines cannot handle within the available budget.

The approach resembles mathematical proof in Lean, but SVA does not require a complete derivation from axioms or a formal derivation of every lemma from its dependencies. It also supplies more than a binary success or failure signal: the time required to discharge an assertion provides a graded measure of proof quality.

These differences suggest that LLM-based helper-assertion generation may be at least as feasible as LLM-based proof generation in Lean, where reinforcement learning has already produced strong results. AlphaProof reached silver-medal performance on IMO problems \cite{deepmind2024alphaproof}. The DeepSeek-Prover series trained on proof-assistant feedback \cite{xin2024deepseekproverv15} and later applied reinforcement learning to subgoal decomposition \cite{ren2025deepseekproverv2}, a task closely related to generating helper assertions.
