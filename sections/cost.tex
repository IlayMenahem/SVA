\section{Cost Estimate}
\label{sec:cost}

We estimate the compute cost of the full pipeline --- staged fine-tuning
(Section~\ref{sec:finetuning}), environment bring-up, and the PPO run
(Section~\ref{sec:rl}) --- against Verda spot prices.\footnote{Verda spot
pricing, retrieved 2026-06: B200 SXM6 at \$2.14/GPU-hour and CPU at
\$0.0025/core-hour (\url{https://verda.com/products}).}

\subsection{Assumptions}
\label{sec:cost-assumptions}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Hardware.} A single B200 SXM6 (\$2.14/GPU-hour spot) for all
    GPU work; proof workers on CPU (\$0.0025/core-hour spot).
    \item \textbf{Model.} Qwen3.6-36B-A3B (MoE, $\sim$$3$B active), served in
    FP8 ($\sim$$36$\,GB of weights).
    \item \textbf{Fine-tuning is parameter-efficient (LoRA).} The combined
    corpus is $\sim$$130$k samples and the two curriculum stages run
    $\sim$$3$ epochs in total, for $\sim$$0.78$\,B training tokens.
    \item \textbf{Rollout generation.} $\sim$$4{,}000$ generated tokens per
    environment step at an aggregate FP8 decode throughput of
    $\sim$$24{,}000$ tokens/s; PPO policy and value-function updates add
    $\sim$$25\%$ on top of generation GPU-time.
    \item \textbf{Proofs.} One formal proof per environment step, $\sim$$30$\,s on
    a single CPU core.
\end{itemize}

These figures assume a clean run; budgeting a $1.5\times$ contingency for
failed runs and iteration gives a planning range of $\sim$\$230--\$377. The
estimate is most sensitive to total environment steps and tokens-per-step,
which scale the RL GPU term linearly.
