\section{Fine-tuning}
\label{sec:finetuning}

Before reinforcement learning, we fine-tune the base LLM on a domain corpus to teach it SystemVerilog, SVA, and the structure of assume-guarantee contracts (Section~\ref{sec:rl}).

\subsection{Data augmentation}
\label{sec:ft-augmentation}

We augment each verified (specification, contract) pair in two ways:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Identifier renaming}: substitute signal, port, and module names with sampled alternatives that follow the design's naming conventions.
\item \textbf{Syntactic paraphrasing}: use rules to produce semantically equivalent SystemVerilog and SVA variants, for example by reordering conjuncts or applying De Morgan's laws and equivalent temporal operators.
\end{itemize}
