\subsection{Curriculum learning}
\label{sec:curriculum}

\textbf{Difficulty grading.} Each pair receives a static difficulty proxy based on the cone-of-influence state-bit count, hierarchy depth, and the property's temporal depth (the nesting of \texttt{\#\#}, \texttt{|->}, and unbounded operators). We calibrate the proxy against the baseline single-shot proof time and timeout rate, then divide the instances into tiers.

We use adaptive curriculum learning~\citep{bengio2009curriculum} to introduce more complex instances gradually.
